GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Are items currently available for real rental?

The catalogue currently includes sample demo listings. Live student listings appear after Supabase is connected and configured.

Can I list my own item?

Yes. Saving a listing requires a verified RentRelay account and the connected Supabase database.

Does RentRelay collect payments?

No. Online payments and confirmed booking requests are not connected yet. Inspect an item and agree on rental terms before handover.

How is student verification handled?

The current setup uses email verification. A mobile number collected during signup is not SMS-verified yet.
