Know the details before you meet.

Review the item, daily price, condition and rental dates before arranging a campus handover.

  1. Read the listingCheck item details, condition, price per day and pickup arrangements.
  2. Agree on datesConfirm the start date, rental duration, return date and total amount with the owner.
  3. Inspect togetherCheck the item at handover and record any existing marks or issues.
  4. Return responsiblyReturn it on time in the agreed condition and keep a record of the handover.
GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Are items currently available for real rental?

The catalogue includes clearly labelled demo examples and any live community listings submitted by users. A listing is not verified and availability is not confirmed.

Can I list my own item?

Yes. You can submit an item with a photo and pickup location. Listings are public; anonymous submissions are allowed and ownership is not verified.

Can I book or pay for an item here?

Not yet. The date calculator only estimates the rental cost. It does not notify the owner, create a booking, reserve availability or collect payment.

How is student verification handled?

The current setup uses email verification. A mobile number collected during signup is not SMS-verified yet.

BUILT AROUND ACCOUNTABILITY

RentRelay is currently a public listing catalogue. Identity checks, booking requests, owner approvals, handover records and payments are not live yet.
