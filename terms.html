Rules for using RentRelay to discover, list and arrange short-term rentals between users.

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Version: 2026-11-14
Important: This is a working draft, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Before public/commercial launch, the operator must publish its verified legal name, address, customer-support and grievance-officer details and obtain review from an Indian lawyer. Details have not been invented.

1. Acceptance and eligibility

By registering or using account-only features, you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Notice. Signup requires an active, unticked checkbox; we record the accepted version and time. You must be legally capable of entering into a contract under applicable Indian law. Provide accurate information, keep your email secure, do not impersonate others or share authentication links or codes. Email confirmation verifies email only; mobile numbers and student status are not independently verified.

2. Nature of the marketplace

RentRelay is intended as a peer-to-peer marketplace. It does not own, sell, lease, insure, deliver or guarantee user-listed items. Any rental agreement is directly between the Lender and Renter. Demo catalogue entries are examples, not real offers. Signed-in users can send a request for a member-owned listing. The owner may accept or decline it; an accepted request is the only time both participants can reveal their saved contact details. RentRelay does not collect payment or guarantee handover. A request is not a confirmed rental until the owner accepts and both users separately agree the final terms. Contact phone numbers are revealed only to the renter and listing owner after acceptance. Mobile numbers are collected at signup but are not SMS-verified. Payments, deposits, escrow, refunds, in-app messaging, ratings, identity verification and dispute handling are not implemented. Live listings require configured Supabase services.

3. Lender/listing-owner responsibilities

  • List only items you own or are authorised to rent and that are lawful and safe.
  • Never list stolen, counterfeit, recalled, dangerous, prohibited, restricted or unlawful goods.
  • Keep descriptions, photos, condition, availability, price, accessories and pickup details accurate; disclose defects and safety issues.
  • Maintain and clean items, explain safe use, and remove/update unavailable listings.
  • Upload only content and photos you have rights and permission to use; do not expose another person's private information.
  • Agree all charges, dates, deposits, late fees and cancellation terms before handover. Do not impose unlawful or discriminatory terms.

4. Renter responsibilities

  • Review item details, condition, price, pickup point and dates before agreeing.
  • Inspect at handover and record existing damage/accessories with consent.
  • Use the item lawfully, carefully, for its intended purpose and under safety/campus rules.
  • Do not sub-rent, lend, modify or misuse without permission.
  • Return on time, at the agreed place, with accessories and in the condition received except ordinary wear.
  • Promptly report loss, damage, malfunction, theft or accidents.

5. Prices, payments, deposits and cancellations

Displayed daily prices are user-provided and may exclude deposits, delivery, late fees or other charges. Users must agree in advance on total price, duration, payment, deposit, late return, cancellation and refunds, and keep records. RentRelay does not collect payments, hold deposits, provide escrow, issue refunds or guarantee payment. Offline payments are directly between users and at their risk, subject to law. Never share card details, UPI PINs, OTPs or banking passwords. Nothing here removes statutory consumer or refund rights.

6. Prohibited conduct

Do not use the Platform for illegal goods or activity, including stolen/counterfeit property, weapons, explosives, controlled drugs, prescription medicines, prohibited alcohol/tobacco, hazardous chemicals, surveillance devices or forged documents. Fraud, scams, harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, malware, scraping and unauthorised access are prohibited.

7. Safety and handovers

Meet in public, well-lit campus locations where permitted; consider bringing a friend. Do not disclose passwords, OTPs, government ID numbers or banking credentials. Do not meet or transact if unsafe or pressured. Users arrange inspection, handover, transport and return directly; RentRelay does not supervise or verify these events.

10. Privacy and personal data

Signup currently collects name, mobile and email. Email confirmation links verify email; mobile is not SMS-verified. Listing details, general pickup-area text and uploaded photos may be public. Exact home, hostel-room or private address details must not be entered. The private address field is not exposed through the public listings API. Signup mobile numbers are not displayed publicly; the phone numbers of both participants are made available to each other only after an owner accepts a booking request. Do not put your home address or sensitive details in public listings. Supabase provides authentication, database and image storage. Data is used for account operation, listings, security, support and legal compliance, and retained only as reasonably necessary.

Do not submit Aadhaar, PAN, bank passwords or UPI PINs.
